## Forensic disposition — independent-review findings

| Finding | Disposition | Evidence | Required change |
|---|---|---|---|
| MAX17048 QSTRT / U6 pin 6 left NC | **REJECTED** | Exact fitted MPN is ADI `MAX17048G+T10`. Its pin 6 is `QSTRT`, an active-low quick-start input, not ground. Pulling it to GND holds/continually asserts quick-start; it must not be grounded when unused. Rev C PCB net is `unconnected-(U6-Pin_6-Pad6)`. | Keep QSTRT explicitly NC, but replace Rev C's generic `Conn_01x09` U6 symbol with a correctly named MAX17048 symbol and an explicit no-connect marker. Pin 5 `ALRT` remains NC by product choice. |
| TPS61232 U5/L2 local power layout | **CONFIRMED** | Rev C coordinates: U5 `(52,69)`, L2 `(62,69)` — 10 mm center-to-center. The `BOOST_SW` F.Cu route runs from U5 pads around `(50.24,68)` through `(52.51,64.95)` to `(63.75,64.95)` and L2 at `(64.35,69)`: about 15 mm of exposed switch-node copper. C20 input is `(44,64)`, C21 output `(44,69)`, and C22/C23 output `(69,64)/(69,69)`; none is local to U5. This contradicts TI's required compact input loop, switch loop, output loop, and small SW copper area. | Re-place U5, L2, C20/C21 and their return vias as a single local cluster. Place the input ceramic from VIN-to-PGND immediately at U5, L2 immediately at SW, output ceramics immediately at VOUT-to-PGND, and remove the long SW trace/copper. Keep the cluster away from GNSS, RF and antenna keepouts. |
| AP63203 U3/L1 local power layout | **CONFIRMED** | Rev C coordinates: U3 `(79,69)`, L1 `(89,69)` — 10 mm center-to-center. The existing `BUCK_SW` path spans U3 `(80.14,69)` to L1 `(86.65,69)` through a 5.1 mm horizontal section plus detour. Its nominal input capacitors C24/C25 are `(104,43)/(104,47)`, 33–36 mm away; the 3V3 bulk capacitors C3/C4 are `(96,62)/(96,66)`, 11–18 mm away. | Re-place U3, L1, input ceramic, bootstrap capacitor, output ceramics, feedback and ground vias as one cluster. Keep SW short, do not route feedback beside SW, retain a solid In1 GND return and thermal copper. |
| Native USB D+/D− route is a defensible 90 Ω pair | **CONFIRMED DEFECT** | Rev C stackup puts F.Cu 0.18 mm over In1 GND, but the pair is not retained on that layer. Both connector nets move through F.Cu/In2/F.Cu, with several independent vias; the two connections have materially different topologies and lengths. `USB_D_P_CONN` alone has two vias and 10.7 mm on In2; D− has a different 6.75 mm In2 leg. D+/D− from ESD to ESP32 have separate In2 detours of about 26 mm and 40 mm. In2 is the POWER_PLANE, not an adjacent solid GND reference. A 0.25 mm width does not prove 90 Ω differential impedance. | Route a new tightly coupled pair from receptacle → USB ESD → series pads → ESP32 on F.Cu directly above the unbroken In1 GND plane. No signal vias; equal topology/length; no stubs; short ESD connection at the receptacle. Specify the differential target and require the fabricator to field-solve the actual stackup before production. |
| ESP32-S3-WROOM-1 PCB antenna environment | **PARTIAL / NOT FABRICATION-READY** | U1 is at `(72,29)`. Its all-copper keepout is correctly encoded on all copper layers as x=48–96, y=1.25–22.25, but the 48×21 mm base-board laminate remains beneath the module antenna and its y=1.25 edge is only 1.25 mm from the board perimeter. Espressif requires the module antenna outside the base board where possible; otherwise it requires a real cutout beneath/alongside it. It also recommends 15 mm enclosure clearance. Rev C enclosure clearance record does not prove this antenna-specific 15 mm free volume. | Make a board-edge antenna notch/cutout so all layers and laminate under the antenna are absent, retain a ground-via fence only on the board side of the feed, and create an enclosure RF cavity/opening with ≥15 mm clearance from plastic, battery, flex antenna, display cable, SMA bodies and fasteners. Re-run PCBA/enclosure geometry from the updated PCBA STEP. |
| RX5808-2012-12P footprint, integration and procurement | **PARTIAL** | The Rev C footprint has 12 pads and a 28×23 mm Fab outline. Its pad numbers map to the referenced 2010 RX5808 V1.0 drawing: 1 GND, 2 ANT, 3 GND, 4–6 CH1–CH3, 7 GND, 8 +5 V, 9 RSSI, 10 audio, 11 VIDEO, 12 GND. RF3 PCB pads agree. Its 5 V draw is specified 170–180 mA. However no qualified supplier/lot or incoming test record exists, and pad 11 VIDEO is presently NC. | Retain only exact `RX5808-2012-12P` modules which pass incoming dimensional, pin-continuity, supply-current, channel, RSSI and composite-video tests. Prohibit "RX5808-like" substitution. Add a buffered 75 Ω VIDEO OUT and sync-valid path to the actual pad 11 VIDEO. DNP RF3 or use customer-supplied/consigned qualified lot when PCBA procurement cannot provide traceability. |

## Additional confirmed defects

| Finding | Disposition | Evidence | Required change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fuel-gauge schematic symbol obscures pin semantics | **CONFIRMED** | `01_power.kicad_sch` instantiates U6 as `Connector_Generic:Conn_01x09`, despite the fitted MAX17048 footprint and MPN. Pin numbers happen to map to the footprint, but review/ERC cannot reason about QSTRT or ALRT electrical intent. | Replace with a native named MAX17048 9-pad symbol and correctly typed pins before running ERC. |
| Analog FPV confirmation absent | **CONFIRMED** | RX5808 VIDEO is pin 11 in the legacy drawing and RF3 pad 11 has an explicit unconnected net. Firmware reads only RSSI. | Add a properly loaded video buffer, 75 Ω VIDEO OUT, and composite-sync separator/logic signal. Firmware may emit `ANALOG_FPV_CONFIRMED` only after a qualified sync train. |
| Present 5.8 GHz monitor does not cover modern 5.x links | **CONFIRMED** | RX5808 is limited by its published 5705–5945 MHz eight-channel table. It observes no activity below 5705 MHz and no usable digital-link occupancy information outside those eight channels. | Add a separately specified 5.1–6.0 GHz passive observation path with real band selection; do not represent it as an SDR or a spectrum analyzer. |
| Rev C power budget is not a Rev C.1 budget | **CONFIRMED** | Rev C manifest records a single 3.3 V calculated peak of 1350 mA. It does not allocate a 5.x detector, video path, current-limited expansion load, their startup peaks, or thermal/ripple margin. | Recalculate and independently review both rails, charger input limit, 1S battery current, boost thermal margin, and SkyPort allocation before sourcing parts. |

## Primary design evidence used

- Analog Devices, **MAX17048/MAX17049 datasheet**, `MAX17048G+T10`: QSTRT is a quick-start/reset input; it is not an unused-ground pin.
- Texas Instruments, **TPS6123x datasheet** and **SLVA638, Five Steps to a Good PCB Layout of the Boost Converter**: minimize the high-current and switch-node loops; place input/output capacitors at the IC ground return.
- Diodes Incorporated, **AP63200/AP63201/AP63203/AP63205 datasheet**: place input capacitor at VIN/GND, minimize SW, keep feedback away from SW and provide thermal ground.
- Espressif, **ESP32-S3 Hardware Design Guidelines**, §§1.4.4, 1.4.7 and 1.4.8: module antenna outside/cut out from the base board; 15 mm enclosure clearance; 90 Ω USB differential impedance, equal parallel routing, minimal vias and a continuous ground reference.
- RX5808 **PRODUCT SPECIFICATION V1.0, 2010-09-02**: 12-pad pin table, 28×23×3 mm envelope, 5 V supply, 170–180 mA current, 75 Ω / 1 Vp-p VIDEO, and the eight-channel frequency table.

No row above is a script-derived pass. The source coordinates and routing descriptions were inspected in the current native Rev C PCB.
